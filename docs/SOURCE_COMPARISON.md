# Comparative reference analysis — 2025 vs 2026

## Source roles

- **2026 editable sponsorship PPTX (15 slides):** base of all proposed generated sponsorship decks, including a company-specific commercial example at slide 13.
- **2026 editable portfolio PPTX (16 portrait slides):** stable source for the team's institutional history, vehicle technical description and public record. It is **not** an interchangeable slide design template.
- **2025 sponsorship PDF (20 pages):** historic reference for sponsorship narrative and topics, not the canonical current-cycle commercial offer.

## Observed differences

The 2025 PDF includes a more detailed vehicle-component and sponsorship narrative, as well as older contacts, sponsor listings and a tax-deduction paragraph. The 2026 PPTX provides newer team metrics and a concise 15-slide visual presentation, including an example-specific support amount and purpose. Historical names, values and benefits must not be promoted to current official information without an owner check.

## Policy implications

1. 2026 PPTX dictates *presentation identity and slide layout*.
2. Institutional facts are versioned by year/cycle; never compare historical figures without labeling cycles.
3. 2025 text may suggest opportunities to research, but cannot authorize current prices, donors' tax treatment, promised benefits or current contacts.
4. Any deduction or incentive tax claim must be reviewed by qualified legal/accounting owners before use; default is **omit tax-saving claims** from auto-generated decks.
5. Business case slides must not reproduce example-specific budgets, screenshots or names automatically.
